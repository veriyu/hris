# Project Genesis: Veriyu HRIS & Cooperative System

## The Origin
The `veriyu/hris` project was conceived from the real-world operational challenges of managing both human resources and cooperative finances. As organizations grow, the separation between HR management (attendance, payroll, employee data) and cooperative activities (savings, loans, member transactions) creates data silos, manual reconciliation efforts, and opaque cash flows.

## The Problem
- **Disconnected Systems**: Employees manage attendance in one app, payroll in another, and cooperative loans via spreadsheets or a separate legacy system.
- **Lack of Field Context**: Many existing solutions are built by developers without field experience, resulting in workflows that look good on paper but fail during daily operations (e.g., handling complex loan interest calculations, partial payments, or overtime adjustments).
- **Data Integrity Risks**: Manual synchronization between HR payroll deductions and cooperative loan repayments leads to errors and distrust among members.

## The Vision
To build a unified, automated, and highly reliable system using **Laravel** and **Filament 5**. The system will seamlessly integrate core HR functions with a robust cooperative management module. 

## Core Objectives
1. **Single Source of Truth**: Centralize employee data, attendance records, and cooperative membership profiles.
2. **Automated Financial Workflows**: Link payroll deductions directly with cooperative loan repayments and mandatory savings.
3. **Operational Transparency**: Provide clear, accessible reports (PDF/Excel) for management and cooperative members.
4. **Developer Experience**: Establish a sustainable, test-driven, and maintainable architecture that adheres to a strict 11-step development workflow, ensuring long-term project health.
