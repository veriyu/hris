## Preferred Architecture

I prefer business applications that follow clear separation of concerns.

Implementation workflow:

1. Database Schema
2. Enum
3. Model
4. Service
5. Action
6. Policy
7. API
8. Filament Resource
9. UI
10. Test
11. Refactor

Business logic should not live in Controllers or Filament Resources.

Complex business rules should be implemented in Services and Actions.
